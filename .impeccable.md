## Design Context

### Users
Business leaders, founders, venture-capital firms, and operators considering Vaibhav for a part-time operating role, advisory engagement, consultancy, partnership, or new venture. They need to assess strategic range, technical depth, credibility, and fit quickly before starting a conversation.

### Brand Personality
Credible, inventive, and quietly authoritative. The voice should feel like a founder-operator and trusted thought partner: intellectually serious, technically capable, commercially aware, and comfortable working across disciplines.

### Aesthetic Direction
A refined editorial profile with contemporary interactive craft. Warm ochre, a sans-only variable typography system, asymmetric composition, and one restrained Codrops-inspired shader. It must remain readable, accessible, responsive, and excellent as a one-page printed resume. Avoid serif typefaces, conventional corporate templates, developer-dashboard aesthetics, gratuitous effects, and startup-gradient cliches.

### Design Principles
- Establish trust before spectacle; interaction should reward attention without competing with content.
- Present breadth as a coherent founder-operator narrative, not a list of disconnected skills.
- Use disciplined sans-serif typography and generous hierarchy to make strategic judgment feel visible.
- Keep every detail legible across phone, tablet portrait and landscape, desktop, and a clean one-page print version.
- Prefer one memorable gesture executed precisely over many decorative flourishes.
